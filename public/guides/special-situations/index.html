When the property situation is complicated.

You may be managing a property you do not live in—or one whose ownership and access are unsettled. Separate authority to arrange work from the government’s compliance deadline.

By Fix My ViolationLast reviewed: October 7, 2026

The short answer

Start by confirming the responsible party, who can authorize entry and which government or association issued the notice. Tell the officer about access or ownership obstacles promptly. They do not automatically suspend the case.

Absentee and out-of-state owners

Verify the property address and parcel number, then obtain every page of the notice. Make sure the enforcing agency has a usable contact method for the responsible party. Do not assume that not seeing a posted notice means no deadline is running; Florida provides several notice methods in § 162.12.

Arrange a local contact, lawful gate access and a clearly defined scope. Ask for photos before authorizing work and after completion. Send the officer the evidence, request reinspection and keep the written outcome. Recurring maintenance is especially useful when no one is regularly checking the property.

Inherited property and estates

Identify the personal representative or other person with legal authority to authorize the cleanup and contract for work. Give the officer a concise account of any probate or access issue and ask what documentation they need. A relative’s involvement alone does not establish authority.

Locate existing notices, balances and liens before assuming this is a new case. Keep invoices and communications with the estate records. For responsibility for a deceased owner’s debts, disputed title or probate deadlines, consult the estate’s lawyer.

Rentals: owner, tenant and manager

A lease may assign yard work to the tenant, but it does not necessarily determine whom the government can enforce against. Some ordinances impose duties on owners, occupants or other responsible persons. Read the cited section and the lease separately.

Notify the owner or manager promptly. Arrange lawful access and required tenant notice before a crew enters. Document the condition and agree on who is authorizing and paying for the work. We do not decide lease disputes or whether a particular entry is legally permitted.

Vacant lots and natural areas

Vacancy is not a general exemption from maintenance. At the same time, an overgrowth notice is not permission to clear every tree or wet area. Ask the officer to identify the affected portion and any applicable agricultural, conservation or landscaping exception.

Mark boundaries, identify soft ground, utilities and hidden dumping, and confirm disposal arrangements. Obtain any required permissions for protected resources before clearing. For ordinary mowing, the immediate goal is the cited condition, not a change to the land’s use or drainage.

Under contract or in foreclosure

Give the title professional and buyer the known notices, orders and recorded-lien information early. Under § 162.06(5), certain transfers during a pending enforcement proceeding have explicit disclosure and government-notification requirements. The case does not simply disappear when ownership changes.

A foreclosure filing does not by itself tell you who can authorize entry or who currently owns the property. Verify the records and obtain legal advice where necessary. Hillsborough County also has property-registration and preservation ordinances that may matter for a foreclosed or vacant property.

Set the cleanup date with time for reinspection and any title requirements. A contractor invoice is not a lien satisfaction.

HOA notices are a separate track

A homeowner association’s lawn notice generally arises from private governing documents and applicable law, not automatically from Chapter 162. HOA appearance requirements, deadlines, hearings and remedies can differ from city or county rules.

Pinellas County and Manatee County explicitly distinguish their code enforcement role from HOA enforcement. Correcting a government violation may still leave an HOA issue; satisfying the HOA may not close a government case.

Send completion evidence to each issuing body and obtain separate confirmations. Ask a Florida lawyer about disputed association fines, liens or enforcement rights.
