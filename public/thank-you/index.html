Your next steps

What happens after you request help.

If you submitted a request, keep your notice handy. Need to confirm receipt? Call 813-671-2757.

Keep the deadline on your calendar.

An online request is not a confirmed booking. We’ll discuss the scope and availability before scheduling. Ask the issuing officer directly about an extension if one is needed; do not assume the date has changed.

Have these details ready.

  • The complete notice and case/citation number.
  • Property address, access instructions and any known hazards.
  • Compliance deadline and hearing date.
  • Areas and conditions the officer says must be corrected.

After the cleanup, send the officer:

  • Dated before-and-after photos showing every cited area.
  • The cleanup invoice and completion date.
  • Your address and case number.
  • A request for reinspection and written confirmation of compliance.

Ask whether any fines, lien, hearing or other violation remains. Keep the written response with your records.
